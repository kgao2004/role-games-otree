A coordination game. Each round you and the other people in your group secretly choose an action, and the actions combine for different payoffs. Your goal is to maximize your group's points.

Actions

🦌  Hunt Stag
Worth 4 points, but only 1 per round.
Risky: needs ≥ 2 players to pay off.
🐇  Hunt Hare
Worth 1 point, but plenty available.
Safe: always pays off.
